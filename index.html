ABOUT

I sit between analysis and engineering.

My strongest work usually starts with a messy operational problem rather than a model specification. I like figuring out what the decision is, what data can actually support it, and what system needs to exist for that answer to remain useful after the first analysis.

I hold a Master’s in Data Science and work across data science, data engineering and increasingly AI engineering. That range lets me move from data quality and architecture, through analysis and modelling, to APIs, dashboards and deployed products.
